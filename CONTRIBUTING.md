# Contribution rules

Contributions are welcome if the uploader has redistribution rights and the source of geometry is described precisely.

- Do not attach or copy commercial STLs, manufacturer CAD/PCB/STEP files, product photographs, screenshots, logos, or copyrighted drawings without explicit rights clearance and a compatible licence.
- Prefer **your own measurements** of named hardware revision with dimensions, tolerances, units, measurement date and measurement method.
- If a model is merely estimated, mark it estimated. Do not describe approximate measurements as exact.
- For externally licensed contributions, provide licence name, source URL, author/copyright notice, required attribution and compatibility review **before** any merge. Anything unresolved stays excluded.
- No brand endorsement claims, graphic logos or suggestion of affiliation.
- Pull requests must identify which meshes/reports change and why. Remove sensitive or personal information.
- Contributions are proposed for release under CC0 1.0 only where the contributor has authority to apply it; otherwise contributions cannot be merged without a separate licence review.
