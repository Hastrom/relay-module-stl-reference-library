# Provenance / sourcing statement

Prepared 2026-10-18.

## AI generation and workflow

- The procedural geometry generator was written by ChatGPT (OpenAI) in this conversation, at the user's request.
- The bundled `*.stl` meshes and `*.csv` geometric output were generated with procedural Python geometry; these are **not copies of externally obtained mesh/STEP/PCB assets**.
- A prior Arduino UNO/Mega task used user-provided official EAGLE archives. **Neither those EAGLE files nor meshes derived from them are included here.**
- Public product descriptions/data sheets were used as factual leads for some numeric **nominal** measurements; those sources are linked in `data/accuracy_sources.json`. A hyperlink is not a statement that a website licenses its content for reuse.
- No third-party photos, CAD files, logos, screenshots, text excerpts or engineering drawings have been incorporated into this release.
- Source URLs are provenance references, **not redistributed third-party content**. Rights in third-party websites/materials remain with their holders.
- The generator and all original model data here are AI-generated and could have geometric similarities to common PCB products, because many relay module families share electrical package sizes and common pitch standards.

The publishing rights holder licenses/dedicates **only rights they lawfully control** in the included original model assets/source. This does not create rights in manufacturer PCB designs, industrial designs, trademarks, patents, or independently-owned creative works.

## Confidence

**No factory PCB coordinates were imported.** Most positions and several hole diameters are estimates. The QA report is about computational mesh validity, not metrological verification.
